# MyanMart — Project Overview

## Goal
Build a Myanmar-first multi-vendor e-commerce marketplace where sellers can register, list products, manage orders, and sell online, while buyers can discover products, place orders, and purchase from multiple sellers.

## Target Users
- **Buyers:** Browse, search, and purchase products.
- **Sellers:** Manage their storefront, products, inventory, and orders.
- **Admins:** Manage users, sellers, products, and marketplace operations.

## Product Principles
- Simple and mobile-first.
- Burmese and English language support.
- Myanmar Kyat (MMK) as the default currency.
- Support local payment methods such as KBZPay, WavePay, and bank transfers.
- Easy for non-technical sellers to use.
- Start with an MVP and expand based on real customer needs.

## MVP Success Criteria
- Sellers can register and publish products.
- Buyers can search products and place orders.
- Sellers can manage incoming orders.
- Buyers can submit payment information and track order status.
- Admins can manage sellers, products, and users.

## Out of Scope
- Native mobile applications.
- Automated delivery integrations.
- Complex recommendation algorithms.
- Microservices.
- Advanced warehouse and accounting systems.
